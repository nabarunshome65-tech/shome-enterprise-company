06 — Responsible business

Clear principles.
Responsible business.

Our working standards are designed to support fair dealings, safe projects, responsible handling of information and transparent business relationships.

01◇

Ethics & fair dealing

We aim to act honestly, avoid misleading claims, disclose relevant conflicts of interest and reject bribery, kickbacks and improper inducements.

02▤

Contracts, billing & records

We aim to agree scope, prices, timelines and change requests in writing, issue appropriate invoices, maintain business records and meet applicable tax and filing duties.

03◈

Privacy & confidentiality

Customer, employee and vendor information should be used only for legitimate business needs, shared on a need-to-know basis and handled with reasonable safeguards under applicable data-protection requirements.

04＋

People, respect & safety

We aim to maintain a respectful workplace, prohibit harassment and discrimination, and use appropriate site-safety practices. Applicable employment, wage, social-security and POSH obligations must be followed where they apply.

05⌁

Vendors & subcontractors

We seek clear work orders, agreed deliverables, transparent payment terms and fair professional conduct. Partners are expected to follow relevant site rules, safety instructions and applicable laws.

06✓

Licences & legal compliance

We aim to maintain registrations, permits, records and statutory filings required for the relevant business activity, location, workforce and contract, and to review requirements when the business changes.

i
Important compliance note

This section summarises intended business standards; it is not a declaration that every statutory requirement applies to, or has already been satisfied by, Shome Enterprise. Indian legal obligations depend on the entity type, turnover, employee count, state, activity and contract. Please verify the requirements for your specific business with a qualified CA or legal professional.
